  Today I noticed signs of overfitting in my DDQN model — the accuracy was too perfect during training but wasn’t generalizing well. To address this, I reduced the model complexity and made the simulation environment more dynamic and realistic to increase variability. On the creative side, my team and I collaborated to film a commercial video summarizing our research goals, project direction, and what makes our approach unique.

blockers: |
  The biggest challenge was finding the right balance between simplifying the model and making the simulation environment more clinically useful without destabilizing training.

reflection: |
  Today reminded me that machine learning isn’t just about tuning numbers — it’s about designing systems that reflect real-world complexity. It was also fun to switch gears creatively and film the commercial with my team. These moments help remind me why we’re doing all of this in the first place.
